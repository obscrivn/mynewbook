# Chapter 10 Transformers, Pretrained Models, and Transfer Learning

This week moves from models that read one word at a time to transformers, which let every word look at every other word through attention. You will see how models such as BERT are pretrained on large amounts of text and then reused for new tasks, and how to choose and check a pretrained model for your own work.

Start with the reading, then work through the coding practice in Google Colab. The practice is ungraded: it gives step-by-step instructions and hints, and the solution notebook shows completed code and suggested answers. Focus on the ideas (attention, contextual meaning, the three ways to use a pretrained model, and model cards) rather than on the math. The few formulas appear only in optional notes.
